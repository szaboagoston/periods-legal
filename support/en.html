Periods support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to szaboagoston@icloud.com, or use Settings → Feedback in the app – it adds the app and iOS version for you.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your device and in your own iCloud account. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I share with my partner? Settings → Sharing. Your partner needs an Apple Account and the app.

Why are intimacy entries hidden? They're hidden by default and every time you leave the app. Tap the eye icon to show them, or turn on “Always show intimacy” in Settings.

How can I log an ovulation test result? Show intimate data with the eye icon, select the day in the Calendar, and turn on “Ovulation”.

Periods' forecasts are estimates. The app is not a medical device and is not a method of contraception.
