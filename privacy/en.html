Privacy Policy

Periods for iOS · Effective November 2, 2026

In short: Periods stores all your cycle and intimacy data only on your device and in your own iCloud account. We have no servers, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We, the developers, cannot access this data. The only things that reach us are anonymous crash reports (which you can turn off) and any feedback you choose to email us.

1. Who we are

The app is developed by Ágoston Szabó (“we”). Contact: szaboagoston@icloud.com.

The data you log never reaches us, so we do not process it: you stay in control of it, and iCloud storage is provided by Apple in your account.

2. What you can log

  • period days;
  • ovulation days you record manually;
  • the time of intimacy entries, and whether they were protected;
  • cycle settings (e.g. cycle length, period length, luteal phase).

This may count as health data. The app uses it only on your device to calculate forecasts (expected period, fertile window, ovulation, travel windows).

Per-device preferences (e.g. notifications, language, intimacy visibility) stay on that device only.

3. Where your data is stored

  • On your device, in the app's own storage.
  • In your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit, private database) if you are signed in to iCloud, so your data syncs between your devices. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we have no access to it.
  • Widgets receive your period and ovulation days and cycle settings through a shared app group container on your device. Intimacy data is never passed to widgets.

4. Sharing with another person

If you choose to, you can share your journal with another Apple Account (for example your partner) through iCloud. Every participant of a shared journal can see and edit all of its data, including intimacy entries. You can stop sharing at any time in the app's Settings tab or in your iCloud settings.

5. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device; no server is involved. They are off by default, and by default they are discreet: only neutral text appears on the Lock Screen.

6. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, it sends an anonymous crash report through Google's Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. A report contains a technical description of the error (stack trace), the app and iOS version, the device model, the language setting and a random installation identifier. It never contains cycle, ovulation or intimacy data, your name or email address, and it is not used for tracking. Google processes the reports under the Firebase privacy terms and keeps them for up to 90 days. Legal basis: legitimate interest (fixing bugs in the app). You can turn this off at any time with “Send crash reports” in the app's Settings tab.

7. Feedback by email

“Feedback” in the Settings tab opens your own mail app with a pre-filled message. Besides the text you type, it contains the app and iOS version, the device model and the language setting, but no cycle or intimacy data. You can see and edit everything before sending. We use the email you send (and your email address) only to reply to you and to fix issues, and we delete it on request.

8. What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • We don't show ads or track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't ask for an account, email address or name.

9. Test builds

Test builds that are not installed from the App Store (distributed with Firebase App Distribution) use Google's Firebase App Distribution to tell testers when a new build is available. For this, Google processes the email address the tester was invited with and basic device information. This does not apply to the app downloaded from the App Store.

10. Deleting your data

  • “Delete all data” in the app's Settings tab deletes your journal from your device and from iCloud. For a shared journal, the deletion applies to every participant.
  • You can also delete the data stored in iCloud in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device; the copy in iCloud remains until you delete it there.

11. Your rights

Because your data stays with you and in your own iCloud account, you can access, change and delete it directly. If you have a question or complaint, contact us at the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Hungary: NAIH, naih.hu).

12. Important notice

Periods' forecasts are estimates. The app is not a medical device and must not be used as a method of contraception or as the only tool for planning a pregnancy. Consult a doctor about any health questions.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the updated version on this page with its effective date.
